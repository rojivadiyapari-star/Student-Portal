About Student Portal

The Student Portal is an official digital gateway built to connect students, academic mentors, and university administration in a unified digital ecosystem. It offers a secure, user-friendly interface that brings all essential student services to your fingertips.

Purpose of the Portal

The fundamental mission of the Student Portal is to modernize educational administration and empower students with transparent access to their academic journey. The key objectives include:

  • Centralized Access: Bringing attendance, courses, grades, and announcements into a single dashboard.
  • Transparency: Enabling students to verify course registrations and monitor attendance in real time.
  • Effective Communication: Providing swift dissemination of exam dates, cultural events, and administrative circulars.
  • Paperless Administration: Minimizing physical queues for fee receipts, grade cards, and feedback forms.

Portal Facilities

The Student Portal offers a comprehensive suite of academic and student life facilities:

Academic Tracking

Real-time visibility into attendance records, internal assessment scores, and university semester grade points (SGPA/CGPA).

Course & Assignment Hub

Access subject syllabus outlines, download assignment problem sets, and view submission deadlines.

Campus Event Registrations

Explore upcoming hackathons, technical workshops, cultural celebrations, and register with a single click.

Library & Resource Catalog

Search university library titles, inspect book due dates, and explore subscribed online research repositories.

Information About the College

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is a premier university accredited with an 'A+' grade by NAAC. The university comprises multiple faculties including Engineering & Technology, Applied Sciences, Computer Applications, Pharmacy, and Management.

Chandubhai S. Patel Institute of Technology (CSPIT) is the pioneering engineering constituent institute of CHARUSAT. CSPIT is dedicated to nurturing cutting-edge engineering talent through research-driven curricula, modern laboratory facilities, and active industry-academia collaborations.
