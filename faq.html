Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using the Student Portal, managing your profile, viewing academic progress, and logging in.

1. What is the Student Portal?

The Student Portal is an integrated online platform designed for students to conveniently view their academic records, semester grades, attendance statistics, assignment deadlines, event schedules, and administrative notices in one central location.

2. How do I log into my Student Portal account?

Click on the Login link in the navigation bar. Enter your assigned university username (or enrollment ID) and secure password, then click the Login button.

3. What should I do if I forget my password?

If you cannot remember your portal password, contact the university IT Helpdesk or visit the administration office with your student identity card to initiate a secure password reset.

4. How can I check my attendance and exam results?

Navigate to the Dashboard page. You will find dedicated sections displaying your enrolled courses, real-time subject-wise attendance percentages, and historical semester SGPA/CGPA records.

5. Where can I find information about college events?

Visit the Events page from the navigation bar. You can view all upcoming workshops, coding competitions, hackathons, and cultural festivals along with their dates, venues, and registration buttons.

6. Can I submit suggestions or report portal issues?

Yes! Please visit our Feedback page. You can fill out the feedback form with your name, email, subject, and message. All feedback is directly reviewed by the portal support team.

7. Who can I contact for urgent technical support?

You can visit the Contact page for university helpline numbers, physical campus address, and official departmental email contacts.

Still Have Questions?
