Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is the Student Portal?

The Student Portal is an online platform where students can access academic information and college services.

2. How do I log in?

Enter your username and password on the login page.

3. What can I do on the Student Portal?

You can view notices, events, courses, attendance, results, and other academic information.

4. What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the "Forgot Password" option or contact the administrator.

5. Who can use the Student Portal?

Students, faculty members, and administrators can access the portal based on their login credentials.

6. How can I contact the college?

Visit the Contact page to find the college contact details.
